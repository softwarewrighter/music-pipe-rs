          Slide-transition beds (20–30 s, 5-second cells)

          How to use: every clip is a run of 5-second cells, each starting on a downbeat: motif (0–5 s, opening slide) → bridges (each different: answer, dorian shadow, lift to G, break) → dénouement (last 5 s, ends on the home chord). Take 0–5 for the title, any n…n+5 for separators (cell starts give whole phrases; no cell repeats another), and L−5…L for the closing slide. Each cell is levelled to the same loudness. Click a cell to hear just that window. Cut one: examples/slice-5s.sh work/video-beds/CLIP.wav 10 (or first / last).

          2 · Game background music

          Loopable mood sets

          Each set is four separate seamless loops: A establish → B bed (×2) → C develop → D turnaround → repeat. Every phrase ends on A-sus and every A starts on D, so a player can stop after any phrase and jump to another set's A. Note: the browser's <audio loop> may leave a tiny gap at the loop point; the files themselves loop sample-exactly.

          Tour — all sets back to back (A B C D each), to judge set-to-set transitions

          Fast transitions (no waiting for the phrase to end)

          How the app uses these:
          1. At the next bar line of the current set (bar_seconds in the catalog: 1.7 s at 144 BPM), fade the current phrase out over 0.5–1 s.
          2. At the same moment, start a bridge whose from_sets/to_sets fit. Its lead-in swell covers the fade.
          3. At the bridge's handoff_seconds, start the target set's phrase A. Every bridge ends on A (the dominant), and every A starts on D, so it resolves naturally; the bridge's tail rings underneath.

          Transition demos

          Each demo cuts a set's phrase C at a bar line 6 bars in, fades it, plays the bridge and starts the next set's A at the handoff point, exactly as the app would.
